Target Retinal Pathologies

VisionAI is engineered to screen for the leading causes of preventable blindness worldwide.

Diabetic Retinopathy

Vascular damage to retinal microcirculation characterized by microaneurysms, hemorrhages, cotton-wool spots, and hard exudates.

Glaucoma

Progressive optic neuropathy causing characteristic excavation of the optic disc, cup-to-disc ratio enlargement, and visual field loss.

Cataract

Opacification of the natural crystalline lens leading to light scattering, visual blurring, and altered retinal fundus illumination.

Normal Fundus

Healthy retinal vasculature with distinct optic cup margins, uniform macular pigment, and absence of lesions or vascular anomalies.

ResNet-50 Residual Architecture

Trained on High-Resolution Color Fundus Photography

The VisionAI model leverages deep residual learning with skip connections to overcome vanishing gradients in deep networks. Pre-trained on ImageNet and fine-tuned specifically on curated multi-class ophthalmic datasets with standardized data augmentation.
